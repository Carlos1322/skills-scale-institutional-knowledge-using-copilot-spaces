# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Product Managers: clarify user value, priorities, and acceptance criteria
- Project Managers: coordinate delivery timing, dependencies, and risk updates
- Technical Lead/Architect: align implementation decisions with architecture and standards

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Project Managers: align delivery plans, scope, and milestone communication
- Business Analyst: refine requirements and acceptance criteria
- Stakeholder/Sponsor: communicate priority, trade-offs, and approval moments

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Product Managers: coordinate roadmap alignment and status reporting
- Developers and QA/Testing Lead: surface delivery progress, quality risks, and dependency issues
- Stakeholder/Sponsor: provide executive updates, escalation paths, and decision support

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define the quality strategy for a project, own test planning, and validate that features meet the agreed acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define the quality strategy, test plan, and release readiness criteria
- Create and maintain test cases, regression suites, and manual validation flows
- Coordinate automated and manual testing across sprint and release cycles
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done before sign-off
- Surface quality risks, defects, and release blockers to project leadership
- Partner with developers on bug triage, fixes, and verification

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet customer and business expectations
- Reduce production defects and regressions
- Increase confidence in release readiness and operational stability

### Typical Communication
- Daily or sprint-based quality checks with delivery team
- Test status updates in sprint reviews, standups, and release readiness meetings
- Defect and risk reports for PM and Product leadership

### Key Interactions
- Product Managers: clarify acceptance criteria, user scenarios, and quality expectations
- Developers: collaborate on testability, regression coverage, and bug validation
- Project Managers: communicate quality status, risks, and dependency impacts

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, guide design decisions, and help the team navigate architecture, dependencies, and technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical feasibility during initiation and planning
- Lead architecture discussions and design reviews
- Define standards, patterns, and technical guardrails for delivery
- Identify technical risks, design debt, and dependencies early
- Mentor developers and support solution quality and maintainability
- Review implementation choices for alignment with architectural goals

### Goals
- Maintain technical consistency, maintainability, and delivery quality
- Reduce rework and architectural drift over time
- Proactively surface technical risks before they block delivery

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and technical planning sessions
- Architecture decision records and technical risk updates
- Cross-functional coordination with PMs, developers, and security stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- Developers: provide guidance, review implementation choices, and reinforce standards
- Product Managers: assess feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery constraints
- Project Managers: escalate technical dependencies and resourcing risks
- Security/Compliance Officer: align technical controls and architecture with security requirements

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide strategic direction, fund or sponsor the work, and help confirm that the project is delivering business value. They provide final support for key decisions and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business goals, strategic priority, and success criteria
- Review major plans, milestones, and go/no-go recommendations
- Support cross-functional alignment and decision-making
- Provide executive visibility on project status and resolution of escalated blockers
- Approve release decisions or major scope changes when needed

### Goals
- Ensure the project stays aligned with business value and strategic priorities
- Accelerate decision-making for high-impact issues
- Maintain confidence in the initiative at the leadership level

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and steering updates
- Weekly or monthly stakeholder status summaries
- Escalation communications and decision checkpoints

### Key Interactions
- Project Managers: receive status updates, risk summaries, and escalation support
- Product Managers: align the roadmap to business outcomes and strategic direction
- Delivery team: review outcomes and approve trade-offs at key milestones

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear, actionable requirements. They bridge business context and delivery execution so work is scoped and validated against real user and stakeholder needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather requirements from stakeholders and subject-matter experts
- Document business processes, user needs, and success criteria
- Translate product intent into user stories, workflows, and acceptance criteria
- Facilitate requirement clarification and trade-off discussions across teams
- Support user acceptance testing and validation of outcomes

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity and rework by clarifying requirements early
- Improve alignment between stakeholder expectations and implementation
- Enable reliable validation of delivered value

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User story refinement and acceptance criteria reviews
- Validation sessions during milestone and release readiness reviews

### Key Interactions
- Product Managers: shape problem definition, prioritization, and measurable outcomes
- Developers: clarify scope, edge cases, and expected behaviors
- QA/Testing Lead: define test scenarios and validate business expected outcomes
- Project Managers: support planning, risk tracking, and dependency clarity

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that the project meets security requirements, regulatory obligations, and operational safeguards throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security, privacy, and compliance requirements early
- Help define secure design patterns and control expectations
- Perform or guide security reviews, threat checks, and risk assessments
- Validate that required scans, policies, and controls are in place
- Support mitigation planning for security-related risks and incidents

### Goals
- Reduce security exposure and compliance risk
- Ensure delivery does not introduce avoidable operational or regulatory issues
- Support confident, secure release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Compliance updates during planning and release readiness
- Incident response and remediation coordination when needed

### Key Interactions
- Technical Lead/Architect: align design and implementation with security controls
- Developers: guide secure coding practices and vulnerability remediation
- Project Managers: surface security risk status and blockers
- QA/Testing Lead: incorporate security testing and validation into quality plans

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help teams work effectively, remove obstacles, and improve the quality and consistency of their delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, retrospectives, and other agile ceremonies
- Help teams surface blockers and focus on work that delivers value
- Coach the team on process adherence, collaboration, and continuous improvement
- Track delivery health indicators such as flow, capacity, and team momentum
- Support a healthy, psychologically safe team environment

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and predictability
- Reduce organizational friction and unnecessary delays
- Strengthen continuous learning and iteration habits

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and coaching sessions
- Retrospective follow-ups and process improvement discussions
- Dependency and bottleneck updates with PM and leadership stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- Project Managers: coordinate on delivery cadence, dependencies, and process improvement
- Developers: support team collaboration and continuous improvement practices
- Product Managers: align on stakeholder communication and delivery trade-offs
- Stakeholder/Sponsor: provide transparency on delivery constraints and team health

## Role coverage summary
The OctoAcme project management framework depends on clear accountability across delivery, business, quality, and governance work. This expanded set of personas helps define:
- who decides what to build
- who coordinates delivery
- who defines quality and acceptance
- who guides technical solutions and security posture
- who bridges business intent and implementation
- who keeps the team aligned and improving over time

This structure supports clearer onboarding, better role ownership, and stronger project outcomes.
